# SilkWorm Creation - Instagram Social Commerce & Sales Growth Playbook

## 1. The Core Funnel Strategy
High-ticket ethnic wear is an emotion-driven, visual, and relationship-based purchase. The winning architecture bridges Instagram discovery directly to frictionless mobile checkout or WhatsApp assisted closing:

```
[ Instagram Reel / Story ] 
      │ 
      ▼ (Comment "SAREE" or Tap Link)
[ ManyChat DM Automation / Fast Mobile Store ]
      │
      ├─► Direct 1-Click UPI Checkout (Self-serve)
      │
      └─► WhatsApp 1-on-1 Video Consultation (Assisted High-Ticket Close)
```

---

## 2. High-Converting Instagram Reel Formats
1. **"The 60-Second Drape Test":**
   - Demonstrate how smoothly the pure linen or banarasi tissue saree pleats and falls on a real person.
2. **"Studio Light vs. Natural Sunlight":**
   - Take the saree from indoor showroom light to outdoor daylight to demonstrate exact color authenticity.
3. **"1 Saree, 3 Occasions":**
   - Style a handloom saree for: (1) Office meeting, (2) Festive family dinner, (3) Wedding cocktail.
4. **"Behind the Looms & Showroom Walkthrough":**
   - Film in the SCO 2 Reliance Square store showing the texture of fabrics, stacks of fresh collections, and customer styling sessions.

---

## 3. Automation & Assisted Selling Workflows
* **ManyChat Instagram DM Automation:**
  - Setup keyword triggers (e.g., when a user comments "PRICE", "BEIGE", or "LINK" on any Reel):
    - Instant automated DM: "Here is the direct link to the [Saree Name] with complimentary Fall & Pico: [Link] + Use code 'SILK500' for ₹500 off your first online order!"
* **WhatsApp VIP Concierge:**
  - Route hesitant buyers to WhatsApp for instant 3-minute video verification calls before packing.
