## 2. Comprehensive Gap Analysis: What the Online Store Lacks

### A. Trust & Social Proof Deficit (Lethal for ₹8k–₹12k Price Range)
1. **AI-Generated Image Disclaimers:** Certain PDPs contain disclaimers stating imagery is AI-generated. High-ticket saree buyers (₹8,000–₹12,000) demand authentic fabric textures, zari luster, reverse weave inspection, and real human drapes.
2. **0 Customer Reviews or Photos:** No review widget (Judge.me/Loox), zero customer ratings, zero unboxing or styling photos.
3. **Hidden Offline Showroom:** They possess a physical SCO in Reliance Square, Zirakpur/Panchkula, but do not leverage it as a primary trust anchor on the homepage header or product pages.
4. **Lack of Authenticity Certifications:** No badges for Silk Mark, Handloom Board, or 100% Pure Linen/Silk guarantees.

### B. Missing Core Saree E-Commerce Features
1. **No Fall & Pico Service:** Saree buyers expect complimentary or optional Fall & Pico edging before shipping.
2. **No Blouse Stitching Add-On:** Missing custom blouse tailoring option (bust size, neckline preferences), which is a proven high-margin order-value booster.
3. **No Drape & Fabric Movement Video:** Customers cannot see how stiff or fluid the linen/banarasi fabric falls when walking.
4. **No Daylight vs. Studio Light Comparison:** Silk and tissue sarees look vastly different under artificial studio flash vs. outdoor sunlight. Lack of color confidence prevents checkout.
5. **No Pincode / Delivery Estimator:** Shoppers cannot check if their saree will arrive before an upcoming wedding, pooja, or event.

### C. Checkout & Social Commerce Bottlenecks
1. **No 1-Click Fast Checkout:** Mobile users must endure multi-step Shopify checkout forms instead of seamless 1-Click OTP + prefilled UPI checkout (GoKwik / Fastrr).
2. **No Assisted WhatsApp Video Shopping:** No CTA to "Book a 5-Minute Video Call on WhatsApp to Inspect this Saree".
3. **Thin Catalog:** Only 17 active SKUs online gives the impression of low inventory or an abandoned catalog.
4. **Restrictive 2-Day Return Window:** A rigid 2-day return policy deters first-time online luxury buyers.
