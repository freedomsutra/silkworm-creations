## 🚀 Key Problems Solved & New Features Implemented

### 1. Mobile Performance Boost (Solving the 77 PageSpeed Score)
* Built with **Next.js 14 (App Router)** and static generation.
* Ultra-lean client bundle (~30 kB route size, 117 kB First Load JS).
* Zero layout shift (CLS: 0), instantaneous mobile in-app webview loading for Instagram links.

### 2. High-Ticket Trust & Tactile Validation
* **Studio Light vs. Natural Sunlight Switcher:** Toggle on every product detail modal to inspect actual sunlight shades (eliminating the #1 return reason for ethnic sarees).
* **Drape & Movement Simulation:** Visual drape motion preview showing fabric fluidity and pleating behavior.
* **Silk Mark & Handloom Certification Badges:** Visual assurance of pure linen, tissue silk, and handloom cotton.
* **Flagship Showroom Trust Anchor:** Prominent highlight of the physical showroom in Reliance Square, Zirakpur/Panchkula.

### 3. Assisted Social Commerce (WhatsApp Integration)
* **1-on-1 Video Consultation Modal:** Direct CTA allowing customers to schedule or instantly join a WhatsApp video call to see any saree live from the showroom in natural daylight.
* **Floating WhatsApp Concierge:** Persistent widget with quick options (Video call, stylist chat, Google Maps store directions).

### 4. Frictionless 1-Click Fast Checkout (GoKwik / Fastrr Inspired)
* Eliminates multi-step forms. Instant mobile phone number verification + prefilled delivery address + 1-tap UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR) and Cash on Delivery.
* Celebration confetti upon order confirmation with immediate WhatsApp dispatch tracking.

### 5. Essential Saree Customizations & Delivery Check
* **Complimentary Fall & Pico:** Auto-included on all sarees with tailored border reinforcement.
* **Custom Blouse Tailoring Add-on:** Standard bust sizes (34–42) or custom measurement submission.
* **Live Pincode Estimator:** Real-time delivery ETA calculation with express same-day delivery for Chandigarh, Panchkula, and Mohali.

### 6. Social Proof & Instagram Shopping
* **Shop The 'Gram Carousel:** Interactive reels carousel linking Instagram content directly to instant checkout.
* **Verified Customer Reviews:** Ratings and testimonials from patrons across Chandigarh, Panchkula, Mohali, and NRI diaspora.
